Sustainable scaling requires more than revenue growth - it demands robust financial infrastructure. Here are essential practices:

1. Implement Rolling 13-Week Cash Flow Forecasts

Move beyond monthly forecasts to weekly cash visibility. Identify cash gaps 90 days in advance.

2. KPI Dashboard with Leading Indicators

Track 5-7 key metrics that predict revenue 60-90 days ahead. Cash conversion cycle, sales pipeline velocity, customer acquisition cost.

3. Dynamic Pricing Models

Implement value-based pricing with annual reviews. Capture 20-30% more margin through segmentation.

Growth Stages Require Different Focus:

  • $0-1M: Cash preservation
  • $1-10M: Process scaling
  • $10M+: Capital efficiency
