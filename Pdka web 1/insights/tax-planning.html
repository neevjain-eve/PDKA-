As we enter 2024, businesses face a dynamic tax landscape with new opportunities for optimization. Here are our top strategies:

1. R&D Tax Credits Optimization

Maximize Research & Development tax credits by properly documenting eligible activities. Many businesses leave 30-50% of credits unclaimed due to inadequate documentation.

2. Succession Planning Through Trusts

Utilize irrevocable trusts to transfer business interests with minimal tax impact. Consider grantor retained annuity trusts (GRATs) for high-growth businesses.

3. Qualified Small Business Stock (QSBS)

Section 1202 allows exclusion of up to $10M or 10x basis on gains from QSBS. Ensure proper elections and holding periods are met.

  • Optimize bonus depreciation
