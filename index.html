About the Firm

P. Dilip Kumar & Associates

PDKA is a professional firm of Chartered Accountants committed to its values, quality policy and ICAI Standards. Over twenty-eight years of practice, the firm has built expertise serving clients in India and across nine countries including USA, UK, Australia, Dubai, Singapore, Ireland, Japan and Italy.

Based in Bangalore, the firm offers end-to-end professional services with particular focus on the IT & ITES sector and the Retail industry. The firm is led by Shri. Dilip Kumar P., FCA, CISA, DISA.

DPDPA · India Data Protection Act 2023

DPDPA compliance is a transformation journey, not a last-minute fix.

Full enforcement begins 13 May 2027. The clock is already ticking.

India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA 2023), notified on 13 Nov 2025, mandates consent-based data handling, breach reporting within 72 hours, and data principal rights. Penalties up to ₹250 crore per breach. Organisations must appoint Data Fiduciaries, implement privacy notices, and complete gap assessments before the final deadline.
