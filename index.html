About the Firm

P. Dilip Kumar & Associates

PDKA is a professional firm of Chartered Accountants committed to its values, quality policy and ICAI Standards. Over twenty-eight years of practice, the firm has built expertise serving clients in India and across nine countries including USA, UK, Australia, Dubai, Singapore, Ireland, Japan and Italy.

Based in Bangalore, the firm offers end-to-end professional services with particular focus on the IT & ITES sector and the Retail industry. The firm is led by Shri. Dilip Kumar P., FCA, CISA, DISA.
