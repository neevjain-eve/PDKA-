About PDKA

A Legacy of Financial Excellence

A fast-growing professional firm of Chartered Accountants committed to its values, quality policy and ICAI Standards.

Who We Are

Over the last fifteen years of practice, the firm has created a niche for itself offering a bouquet of services to a selected clientele from India and other countries like USA, Australia, Dubai, UK, Ireland, Singapore, Japan, Italy, and more.

Based in Bangalore, the IT Capital of India, PDKA has made a name for itself by offering end-to-end services under one roof — in particular for the IT & ITES industry and the fastest growing Retail Industry of India.

Our Quality Policy

PDKA's objective is to be a one-stop service provider for corporate houses with strict adherence to the standards, guidelines and regulations of The Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI).

We maintain an efficient and effective Quality Management System (QMS). Quality is the responsibility of all members of staff, individually and collectively.

Systems, processes, service design, delivery and communications are continually improved to conform to client requirements and expectations.

Our Audit Team
