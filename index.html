Who We Are

The firm has been in practice for fifteen years, providing professional services to clients from India and other countries including USA, Australia, Dubai, UK, Ireland, Singapore, Japan and Italy.

Based in Bangalore, the firm offers services to clients in the IT & ITES industry and the Retail sector.

Our People

Leadership

DK

Shri. Dilip Kumar P.

B.Com, FCA, CISA, DISA  ·  Founder & Proprietor

The team at PDKA is led personally by him. He has more than 15 years of post-qualification experience across all areas of CA practice. He has successfully completed the internationally recognised Certified Information Systems Auditor (CISA) and ICAI's Diploma in Information Systems Auditor (DISA), providing expertise in IS Audit.

He specialises in business entry strategies for foreign companies in India, business valuations, setting up internal controls & corporate governance in start-up companies, and providing CFO services to foreign companies.
