GST 2025: ITC Reconciliation, GSTR-2B Changes & E-invoicing Updates

Critical updates on Input Tax Credit reconciliation and mandatory e-invoicing thresholds for FY 2025-26.

The GST framework in India continues to evolve, with several amendments effective from FY 2025-26 that businesses must account for in their compliance processes.

Input Tax Credit — GSTR-2B Reconciliation

  • ITC claims must be fully reconciled with GSTR-2B before filing GSTR-3B.
  • Excess ITC claimed without corresponding GSTR-2B entry will trigger demand notices.
  • Monthly reconciliation is recommended — not just at year-end.

E-invoicing Updates

  • Threshold reduced to ₹5 crore aggregate annual turnover from 1 April 2025.
  • E-invoices must be generated within 30 days of the invoice date.
  • Time limit for reporting invoices on IRP portal strictly enforced.

GSTR Filing Deadlines

  • GSTR-1: 11th of following month (monthly) or quarterly with QRMP scheme.
  • GSTR-3B: 20th of following month for monthly filers.
  • GSTR-9 Annual Return: 31 December for the previous financial year.

Compliance Checklist

  • Enable e-invoicing if your turnover exceeds ₹5 crore.
  • Reconcile GSTR-2B monthly with purchase register.
  • Review reverse charge mechanism (RCM) applicability for your vendor categories.
