ICAI Code of Ethics 2026: What Changed for CA Firms

Key amendments affecting independence, advertising norms, digital practice standards and disclosure obligations.

The Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI) updated its Code of Ethics in 2026, introducing amendments that affect how CA firms operate, market their services, and maintain independence.

Advertising and Marketing Norms

  • CA firms may maintain informational websites listing professional services — no promotional or comparative advertising permitted.
  • Social media presence limited to factual information about the firm and its services.
  • Testimonials, client logos, award mentions, and subjective claims remain prohibited.

Independence Requirements

  • Enhanced documentation requirements for independence assessments before accepting audit engagements.
  • Cooling-off period clarified for partner rotation in statutory audits.
  • Non-audit services restrictions tightened for statutory audit clients above a specified threshold.

Digital Practice Standards

  • Guidance issued on use of technology in audit — AI tools must be supervised by a qualified CA.
  • Cloud storage of client data permissible subject to data security safeguards.
  • Remote working by audit staff requires documented supervision protocols.

Disclosure Obligations

  • Engagement letters must now include data handling and confidentiality clauses explicitly.
  • Disclosure of fees for non-audit services to audit committee where applicable.
