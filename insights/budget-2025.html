Union Budget FY 2026-27: Key Tax Changes for Businesses

Analysis of direct and indirect tax proposals covering corporate tax, TDS, presumptive taxation and MSME provisions.

The Union Budget 2026-27 introduced several significant changes to India's direct and indirect tax framework. This article summarises the key amendments relevant to businesses, particularly in the IT, ITES and Retail sectors.

Direct Tax Changes

  • New income tax slabs under the default regime — revised thresholds provide relief to individuals and HUFs.
  • TDS rate rationalisation — several TDS sections merged for ease of compliance.
  • Presumptive taxation limits revised upward for professionals and small businesses under Section 44ADA and 44AD.
  • Surcharge on long-term capital gains revised for certain asset classes.

Corporate Tax Provisions

  • No change to base corporate tax rate (22% for domestic companies, 15% for new manufacturing companies).
  • MAT (Minimum Alternate Tax) rate remains at 15%.
  • Revised provisions for startup deductions under Section 80-IAC extended.

Indirect Tax / GST

  • Input Tax Credit rules tightened — GSTR-2B matching made mandatory for ITC claims.
  • E-invoicing threshold reduced further — applicable to businesses with turnover above ₹5 crore.

Action Points for Businesses

  • Review your TDS compliance framework in light of merged sections.
  • Assess eligibility for presumptive taxation if you are a professional or small business.
  • Ensure GSTR-2B reconciliation is in place before claiming ITC.
