FEMA 2025: Foreign Remittances, ODI Rules & Compliance for IT Companies

Updated FEMA compliance guide covering overseas direct investment, LRS limits, and key obligations for IT & ITES companies.

India's Foreign Exchange Management Act (FEMA) regulations are frequently updated. This guide covers key compliance obligations for IT and ITES companies with foreign clients, overseas subsidiaries, or international revenue streams.

Liberalised Remittance Scheme (LRS)

  • Annual LRS limit remains at USD 2,50,000 per resident individual per financial year.
  • TCS at 20% applicable on LRS remittances above ₹7 lakh per year (except education and medical).
  • Form 15CA/CB mandatory for most foreign remittances — obtain CA certificate before remitting.

Overseas Direct Investment (ODI)

  • Indian companies may invest up to 400% of their net worth in overseas entities.
  • Annual Performance Report (APR) mandatory for all overseas investments by 31 December each year.
  • ODI in financial services sector requires RBI prior approval.

IT/ITES Companies — Key Obligations

  • Foreign inward remittances (export proceeds) must be realised within 9 months from the date of export.
  • SOFTEX/eBRC filing mandatory for all software exports — file within 30 days of invoice.
  • Transfer pricing documentation required where international transactions exceed ₹1 crore.

Common Non-Compliance Areas

  • Delay in repatriation of export proceeds.
  • Failure to file Annual Performance Report for overseas subsidiaries.
  • Incorrect classification of receipts — capital vs. current account.
