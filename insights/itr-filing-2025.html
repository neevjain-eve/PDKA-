ITR Filing FY 2025-26: Compliance Checklist

Updated guide on ITR forms for AY 2026-27, filing deadlines, new disclosure requirements and penalty provisions.

The Income Tax Return filing season for FY 2025-26 (AY 2026-27) has commenced. This checklist covers all key compliance requirements for individuals, HUFs, firms and companies.

Applicable ITR Forms

  • ITR-1 (Sahaj): Resident individuals with income up to ₹50 lakh from salary, one house property and other sources.
  • ITR-2: Individuals/HUFs with capital gains, foreign assets, or multiple house properties.
  • ITR-3: Individuals/HUFs with business or professional income.
  • ITR-4 (Sugam): Presumptive taxation under Sections 44AD, 44ADA, 44AE.
  • ITR-6: Companies (other than those claiming exemption under Section 11).

Key Deadlines

  • 31 July 2026 — Non-audit cases (individuals, HUFs, firms not subject to audit).
  • 31 October 2026 — Audit cases (companies, firms requiring tax audit).
  • 30 November 2026 — Transfer pricing cases.
  • Belated returns — up to 31 December 2026 with penalty under Section 234F.

New Disclosures for AY 2026-27

  • Disclosure of cryptocurrency/virtual digital asset transactions mandatory in Schedule VDA.
  • Foreign asset reporting expanded — additional fields for beneficial ownership.
  • Director identification and DIN mandatory for company directors.

Penalty for Late Filing

  • ₹5,000 if return is filed after due date but before 31 December.
  • ₹1,000 for taxpayers with total income up to ₹5 lakh.
